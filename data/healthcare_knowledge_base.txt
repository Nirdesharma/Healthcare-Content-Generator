Healthcare patient summaries should include chief complaint, relevant history, examination highlights, assessment, and plan.

Clinical notes should prioritize objective findings, medication reconciliation, allergies, comorbidities, and follow-up recommendations.

For diabetes follow-up, include latest HbA1c trends, fasting blood glucose patterns, current medications, adherence barriers, diet, and physical activity status.

For hypertension documentation, include blood pressure trend, antihypertensive regimen, lifestyle recommendations, and evidence of end-organ involvement when available.

Discharge instructions should clearly state diagnosis, medication changes, warning signs, activity limits, dietary guidance, and when to seek urgent care.

Patient-friendly communication should avoid unnecessary jargon, use short sentences, and include actionable next steps and reassurance.

Professional documentation should maintain neutral, precise language and avoid unsupported conclusions.

When details are unavailable, explicitly mark assumptions and pending investigations.
